# SurroundCore philosophy

SurroundCore exists to make excellent music playback open, durable and user-owned.

## The rules

1. **The complete player is free.** There is no subscription, feature paywall, licence server or deliberately crippled community edition.
2. **Your music and hardware remain yours.** Local playback must not require an external cloud service or vendor account.
3. **Old hardware is not obsolete because its manufacturer lost interest.** If a protocol can still be implemented safely and legally, a maintained community driver is welcome.
4. **Preserve source quality.** The library stores and reports what the source actually is. Conversion happens only when an endpoint requires it, and the UI/API must say so explicitly.
5. **One system, many ecosystems.** Meridian, AirPlay, Cast, Sonos, UPnP/DLNA, ALSA/USB/HDMI and future community transports belong behind one capability model.
6. **No fake support badges.** A transport is Proven, Implemented-Unverified, Experimental or Planned. Documentation must say which.
7. **Local-first and self-hostable.** Core library, queues, users, artwork and local playback keep working without a vendor service.
8. **Interoperability beats lock-in.** Public APIs, documented driver contracts and portable metadata are features, not threats.
9. **Contributors are first-class.** A new device driver or provider adapter should not require understanding the entire server.
10. **Donations are optional.** If someone wants to buy the maintainer a coffee, brilliant. Nothing in the player depends on doing so.
